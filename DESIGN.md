## Brand & Style

This design system translates the warmth, safety, and vibrant wonder of early childhood education into an elevated digital interface. Designed for parents, educators, and young learners, the interface evokes joy, optimism, and approachable warmth while maintaining institutional clarity and modern accessibility.

Drawing inspiration from early learning spaces and tactile, storybook warmth, the design style merges **Playful Tactility** with **Modern Cleanliness**. Softly rounded geometry, generous breathing room, illuminated neutral backdrops, and chromatic accents inspire trust and affection without clutter or overwhelming visual noise.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from the cheerful painted lettering of early childhood school facades:

- **Primary (`#E63946` / Vermelho Coral Brilhante):** Energizing, warm, and inviting. Used for primary CTAs, active highlights, and joyful brand accents.
- **Secondary (`#2B90D9` / Azul Céu Turquesa):** Clear, reliable, and friendly. Balances the warm tones with trust and technological clarity.
- **Tertiary (`#F6C343` / Amarelo Sol Dourado):** Optimistic, creative, and lively. Perfect for celebration cues, badges, and attention-drawing banners.
- **Semantic Accents:**
  - **Verde Esperança (`#2E9A68`):** Success states, attendance confirmations, garden and health activities.
  - **Roxo Violeta Lúdico (`#5E50A1`):** Creative milestones, arts, evening schedules, and secondary navigational tags.
- **Base & Neutral:**
  - Canvas surface: `#FAFCFE` (crystalline, soft white tinted with micro-cool light).
  - Surface cards: `#FFFFFF` (pure elevated crisp white).
  - Neutral text: `#2C3E50` (charcoal navy, gentle on eyes compared to pure black).
  - Subdued text: `#6C7A89`.

## Typography

The pairing between **Quicksand** and **Nunito Sans** produces an intrinsically friendly, approachable, and human demeanor:

- **Headlines & Display (Quicksand):** Rounded terminal geometry creates an open, welcoming rhythm that mirrors childhood innocence and storytelling books.
- **Body & Continuous Copy (Nunito Sans):** Balances organic roundedness with high legibility across dense parent communications, daily diaries, and administrative text.
- **Letter Spacing:** Keep tracking relaxed (+0.01em) in labels and headers to enhance legibility and an open, cheerful air.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid responsive grid model provides comfort, readability, and spacious pacing:

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 2rem outer margins, 1.25rem gutters, max-width contained at 1200px for comfortable reading and focused dashboards.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** 1.5rem margins, 1rem gutters.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** 1rem outer margins, 0.75rem gutters with stacked touch targets.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Built around multiples of 4px/8px. Generous internal card padding prevents information crowding, sustaining an aura of calmness and order for parents and teachers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through **tinted ambient shadows** and **tonal card layering**, avoiding stark, heavy dark shadows in favor of luminous softness:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#FAFCFE`, neutral canvas with subtle micro-tint.
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards & Panels):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with ultra-soft ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(43, 144, 217, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards & Floating Menus):** `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(44, 62, 80, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(44, 62, 80, 0.14)`.
- **Tactile Softness:** Interactive components utilize gentle bottom-border highlights or 1px pastel border definitions (`rgba(43, 144, 217, 0.12)`) instead of heavy contrasting dividing lines.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a friendly, gentle curvature level:

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** 0.5rem (`rounded-md`) to 1rem (`rounded-lg`).
- **Cards & Content Containers:** 1.25rem to 1.5rem (`rounded-xl`), evoking rounded soft-corner building blocks.
- **Avatars, Chips & Pills:** Full circular pill rounding (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for friendly tags, daily schedule indicators, and child profile icons.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* Filled with `#E63946`, text in pure white, pill or 1rem rounded radius with subtle warm drop shadow (`0 4px 12px rgba(230, 57, 70, 0.25)`). On hover, smooth translateY(-1px).
  - *Secondary:* Gentle sky tint fill (`rgba(43, 144, 217, 0.12)`) with `#2B90D9` text.
  - *Playful Alt:* Sunny yellow `#F6C343` with dark slate text for celebratory actions.
- **Chips & Tags:**
  - Pill-shaped with soft tinted pastel backgrounds paired with their vibrant parent token (e.g., Garden Day in `#EAF6EE` background with `#2E9A68` text; Art Studio in `#EFEBFA` background with `#5E50A1` text).
- **Cards:**
  - White surface, 1.25rem rounded corners, 1.5rem internal padding, gentle sky-tint ambient shadow. Optional 4px colored accent bar at top for categorization (homework, meal, play, notes).
- **Inputs & Form Controls:**
  - Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1.5px solid `#E1E8EE`, rounded at 0.75rem. Active focus ring glows gently in Coral or Sky Blue (`0 0 0 3px rgba(43, 144, 217, 0.20)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - 1.25rem circular or rounded boxes with `#2E9A68` (checked green) or `#2B90D9` (checked blue) filled states featuring clean rounded check vectors.
- **Specialized Kid/School Components:**
  - *Activity Badges:* Circular illustrations framed with alternating multi-color borders.
  - *Daily Timeline:* Vertical dotted tracks connected by playful multicolored milestones (coral, sun yellow, emerald, violet).
